# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We aim to make this project welcoming and useful for security, cloud, and engineering practitioners.

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and professional in issues, discussions, pull requests, and reviews.

## Expected Behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experience levels.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Help keep public examples free of real tenant data or sensitive information.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, or real customer/tenant data in public issues or pull requests.
- Deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its users.
